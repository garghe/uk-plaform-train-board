Put the board on your home screen

It opens full screen like an app, with its own icon. Free, nothing to download from the App Store.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (in the address bar or at the bottom of the screen).
  3. Tap View More ⌃ if you don't see the next option, then scroll down.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen ⊞.
  5. Tap Add. The Departures icon appears on your home screen.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Tap Install or Add. The Departures icon appears on your home screen.
